Liver Cutlets

Iron-rich beef & liver cutlets — the liver hidden in the mince, the iron unlocked by lemon & tomato.

About 35 min · makes ~10 · a milder batch — less liver than the 2:1 recipe
Why this heals you
  • Beef liver — nature’s multivitamin: the most concentrated iron, B12 & folate to rebuild the blood you gave to birth
  • Beef mince — heme iron & protein, and it hides the liver so it just tastes like cutlets
  • Lemon & tomato alongside — vitamin C to unlock the iron your body can use
  • Ground pumpkin seeds — zinc & magnesium, quietly folded in for repair
Ingredients
100 gBeef liver (blitzed smooth)
400 gBeef mince
200 gZucchini, carrot & onion (grated, squeezed dry)
1Egg (to bind)
3 tbspDill, chives & coriander (+ a little mint)
to seasonSalt, pepper, dried onion
1 tbspBeef gelatin (holds them together)
2 tbspPumpkin seeds (ground)
1 tbspGhee (to fry)
the sideLemon + 2 tomatoes
Method
1
Blitz the liver · 2 min

Blend 100 g liver until completely smooth — it should pour. This is what hides it.

2
Mix everything · 5 min

Squeeze the water from the grated zucchini, carrot & onion. Fold together with the mince, liver, egg, the herbs, gelatin, ground pumpkin seeds and seasoning.

3
Shape · 5 min

With wet hands, shape the mix into even oval cutlets, all a similar size. Softer than plain mince — that’s right.

4
Cook through · 8 min a side

Pan-fry in 1 tbsp ghee over medium heat, about 8 min each side. Or bake at 190°C for 25 min.

No pink in the middle. Cut one open to check — brown all the way through before serving.

5
Plate

Serve with lemon wedges & sliced tomato and a scatter of the herbs. The vitamin C helps the iron absorb.
