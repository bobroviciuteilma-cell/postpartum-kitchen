Method · step 1 of 3

Warm the stock — not boiling

Gently warm 2 cups meat stock — warm enough to steam, never boiling (boiling scrambles the yolks in the next step).

Done looks like: steaming, no bubbles.

Screen stays on while you make it

Method · step 2 of 3

Blend — yolks, ghee, salt

Into a blender (or a jar for the stick blender): the warm stock, 2 egg yolks, a spoon of ghee and a pinch of salt.

The yolks stay barely cooked. Use the freshest eggs you have (or pasteurised) — and wash hands after separating them.

Done looks like: everything in the jug, yolks whole.

Method · step 3 of 3

Whizz 30 sec — pour it frothy

Blend about 30 seconds, until smooth with a real froth on top. Pour into a mug and take it to Mum warm.
