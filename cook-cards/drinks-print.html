Nettle Tea

About 15 min · makes 1 cup
Ingredients
1–2 tspDried nettle
1 cupJust-boiled water
a squeezeLemon (optional)
Method
1
Steep, covered — 10–15 min

Pour 1 cup just-boiled water over 1–2 tsp dried nettle. Cover the cup and let it steep — longer pulls more minerals.

2
Strain

Strain into Mum's cup. The spent leaves will take a second steep later.

3
Lemon, then take it to Mum

Add a squeeze of lemon if Mum asked — it helps the iron absorb. Take it to her warm.

Jujube & Goji Tea

About 20 min · makes 3 cups
Ingredients
6, splitRed dates (jujube)
1 tbspGoji berries
3 cupsFiltered water
2 slicesFresh ginger (optional)
Method
1
Tear the dates open — 2 min

Tear or slit 6 red dates so each one falls open — this lets the sweetness out into the water.

2
Simmer the dates — 15 min

Drop the dates (and the ginger, if Mum asked) into 3 cups water. Bring to a gentle simmer, lid half on.

3
Goji in — 5 min more

Stir in 1 tbsp goji berries and simmer 5 min more. Pour a cup warm — the softened fruit is for eating too.

Method

Measure the seeds — 1 min

Golden Milk

About 5 min · makes 1 mug
Ingredients
1 cupMilk of choice
½ tspTurmeric
½ tspGinger
a good pinchBlack pepper
a pinchCeylon cinnamon
a pinchCardamom
1 tspGhee
1 scoopCollagen
Method
1
Warm the milk — gently

Warm 1 cup milk in a small pot — steaming, not boiling.

2
Spices in — simmer 3 min

Whisk in the turmeric, ginger, cinnamon, cardamom, a good pinch of black pepper and the ghee. Keep it at the gentlest simmer.

3
Off the heat — collagen in

Take the pot off the heat, then whisk in the collagen scoop (and honey, if Mum asked) until smooth.

Almond, Date & Saffron Milk

About 10 min · makes 1 large mug
Ingredients
8, soakedAlmonds
2, pittedMedjool dates
a pinchSaffron threads
a pinchCardamom
1 cupMilk of choice
Method
1
Soak the almonds — 10 min hot

Soak 8 almonds in hot water for 10 min (or overnight, if planned ahead), then slip the skins off.

2
Blend it silky — 1 min

Blend the almonds, 2 dates, the cardamom and 1 cup milk until completely smooth.

3
Warm it golden — 2 min

Pour into a small pot, warm gently, and crumble in a pinch of saffron. Let it sit 2 min so the gold spreads.

Masala Chai

About 10 min · makes 2 cups
Ingredients
1 tspLoose black tea
1 cupFiltered water
½ cupMilk of choice
3 slicesFresh ginger
3Cardamom pods
1 stickCeylon cinnamon
3Cloves
2Black peppercorns
Method
1
Crush the whole spices — 1 min

Lightly crush the cardamom pods, cloves, peppercorns and ginger — just cracked open.

2
Simmer the spices — 3 min

Into 1 cup water with the cinnamon stick. Boil, then simmer so the water takes the spice.

3
Tea in, milk in — 3 min

Stir in 1 tsp black tea and ½ cup milk. Simmer gently to the colour Mum likes.

4
Strain, sweeten, take it up

Strain into cups. Honey off the heat, only if Mum asked. Morning or early afternoon is its time.

Meat Stock Latte

About 5 min · makes 1 mug
Ingredients
2 cupsMeat stock
2Egg yolks
1 spoonGhee
a pinchSea salt
Method
1
Warm the stock — not boiling

Gently warm 2 cups meat stock — warm enough to steam, never boiling (boiling scrambles the yolks in the next step).

2
Blend — yolks, ghee, salt

Into a blender (or a jar for the stick blender): the warm stock, 2 egg yolks, a spoon of ghee and a pinch of salt.

3
Whizz 30 sec — pour it frothy

Blend about 30 seconds, until smooth with a real froth on top. Pour into a mug and take it to Mum warm.

Morning Waters

About 5 min · makes 1 cup
Ingredients
1 cupWarm filtered water
½ tspAmla powder (optional)
½ tspAjwain seeds (optional)
½Lemon (optional)
a littleHoney (optional)
Method
1
Which water did Mum ask for?

Mum picks one each morning: amla (vitamin C), ajwain (bloating), or lemon (simple). Warm a cup of water — warm, not hot.

2
Stir, steep or squeeze

Amla: stir in ½ tsp until it disappears. Ajwain: steep ½ tsp seeds 5 min, then strain. Lemon: squeeze half a lemon in.

3
To Mum — before food

Take it to Mum 20–30 min before breakfast. A little honey only if she asked.

Dashamoola Bedtime Decoction

About 15 min · makes 1 small cup (30–60 ml)
Ingredients
1–2 tspDashamoola powder
½ cup / 120 mlWater
½ cupWarm milk (optional)
½ tspGhee (optional)
1 tspHoney (optional)
Method
1
Simmer it small — 10–12 min

Stir 1–2 tsp dashamoola powder into 120 ml water in the smallest pan. Simmer gently, uncovered, until it reduces by about half.

2
Strain — a cordial-sized cup

Strain through a fine sieve into a small cup: 30–60 ml of dark decoction. That little cup is the dose — once or twice a day, no more.

3
Milk, ghee & honey — if asked

If Mum asked: top with ½ cup warm milk and ½ tsp ghee; stir in honey off the heat.

4
Warm, after the last feed

Take it to Mum warm, after the last feed of the evening.

Beet Kvass

Hands-on 10 min · ready in 3–5 days · makes ~1 L
Ingredients
3 mediumOrganic beets
1½ level tbspSea salt — non-iodised
to fill the jarFiltered water
a few slicesFresh ginger (optional)
Method
1
Beets & salt into a clean jar

Wash hands and use a clean glass jar. Dice 3 beets (skin on) and fill the jar one-third full. Add 1½ level tbsp non-iodised salt (+ ginger, if Mum asked).

2
Water to 2–3 cm below the rim

Fill with filtered water to 2–3 cm below the rim, and stir once to start the salt dissolving.

3
Cover & wait — 3 to 5 days

Cover with a cloth or loose lid, and leave at room temperature, out of the sun. Once a day: stir, and push the beets back under. Taste from day 3 — it's ready when it's tangy and earthy.

4
Strain into a bottle — fridge

When it tastes right: strain into a clean bottle and refrigerate. Mum has a small glass a day. The beets go on to borscht.
