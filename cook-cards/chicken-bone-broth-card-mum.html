Chicken Bone Broth

Feet & necks simmered low — deep collagen and warm comfort, a cup a day or the base of everything.
Time to make 2–3 hours, mostly unattended
Keep fridge ~4 days · freeze in jars + ice-cube trays
When to drink A warm cup a day, any time — or as the base for soups, khichdi and one-pots. It counts toward your fluids.
Why this heals you
  • Chicken feet & necks — the collagen parts: the pot sets to jelly when cold, and that wobble is the point
  • Glycine-rich and easy to take — warmth and fluids on days eating feels like work
  • Ginger, turmeric & ajwain — the warming, digestion-kind spices
  • The base of the week — khichdi, soups and grains all start from this pot

The broth itself is simply food. The one flag lives in the warming swap: star anise stays culinary — one pod, strained out, Chinese star anise only. .

    to tasteSalt
    Method
    1
    Soak

    Chicken feet, necks and 3 L cold water into the biggest pot, with 2 tbsp cider vinegar. Let it sit — no heat yet.

    2
    Boil & skim

    Bring to a hard boil. Grey foam will rise — scoop it off and throw it away until the surface looks clean.

    3
    Simmer low

    Turn right down to a bare simmer — the odd lazy bubble, never rolling. Add the ginger, turmeric, peppercorns, garlic, ajwain, bay and the herb sprigs. Top up with hot water now and then.

    4
    Season, strain & jar

    Salt to taste. Strain off the bones and pour into jars — some for the fridge, some for the freezer (ice-cube trays make cooking portions). If Mum asked: the red dates + goji go in for the last 15 min before straining.
