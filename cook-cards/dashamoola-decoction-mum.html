Dashamoola Bedtime Decoction

The classic ten-root Ayurvedic postpartum tonic — simmered small, taken warm in the evening. Tradition, honestly labelled.
Time to make about 15 min
Keep make it fresh each time
When to drink Evening, after the last feed — a small warm cup, once or twice a day at most.
Why this heals you
  • Ten roots — THE classic Ayurvedic formula for the 42-day postpartum window
  • Vata-settling — traditionally taken to ground a wired-but-tired nervous system
  • Warm milk & ghee — the classical evening carrier for the bitter roots
  • A cordial-sized cup — kitchen dose, not a megadose

You bought the powder — that's the right form while nursing. The common bottled form, Dashmularishta, is fermented and carries self-generated alcohol; your powder decoction doesn't. .

Your instructions

Made to its small cup — the 30–60 ml pour IS the measure. Nothing to scale.

Make it bedtime (for yourself)

The dose is the small cup itself — 30–60 ml, once or twice a day, made fresh.

    Method
    1
    Simmer down

    Stir 1–2 tsp dashamoola powder into 120 ml water in the smallest pan. Simmer gently, uncovered, until it reduces by about half.

    2
    Strain small

    Strain through a fine sieve into a small cup: 30–60 ml of dark decoction. That little cup is the dose — once or twice a day, no more.

    Small is the point. A cordial-sized pour, made fresh each time — never a mug of it.
    3
    Make it bedtime

    If Mum asked: top with ½ cup warm milk and ½ tsp ghee; stir in honey off the heat.

    4
    Take it up

    Take it to Mum warm, after the last feed of the evening.
