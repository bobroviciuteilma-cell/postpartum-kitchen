Mineral Refresher

Orange juice, coconut water, cream of tartar & sea salt — the electrolytes night feeds drink down. (You may know it as the ‘adrenal cocktail’.)
Time to make about 2 min
Keep drink it fresh
When to drink Mid-morning or the afternoon dip, between meals — not a bedtime drink.
Why this heals you
  • Orange juice — vitamin C & potassium
  • Coconut water + cream of tartar — a genuinely potassium-rich pair
  • A pinch of sea salt — sodium, the other half of fluid balance
  • Why not ‘adrenal’? Because ‘adrenal fatigue’ isn’t a recognised condition — the drink doesn’t need the myth

Breastfeeding is thirsty work — fluids and minerals genuinely matter to how you feel. This glass is hydration that tastes like looking after yourself; reach for it instead of a second coffee. .
