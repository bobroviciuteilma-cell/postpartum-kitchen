Nettle Tea

A deep-green mineral tea — the everyday sip that quietly carries iron, calcium & magnesium.
Time to make about 15 min
Keep brew fresh — the same leaves take a second steep
When to drink Any time, every day. The longer it steeps, the more minerals end up in the cup.
Why this heals you
  • Nettle leaf — genuinely mineral-dense: iron, calcium & magnesium at food level
  • A squeeze of lemon — vitamin C helps the iron absorb
  • One of the classic nursing-mother teas
  • Caffeine-free — an all-day habit

Nettle is a food-level herb in tea amounts and a long-standing nursing tea. The honest read: herbs move milk far less than frequent feeding does — enjoy the cup for its minerals and let the feeding do the heavy lifting. .
