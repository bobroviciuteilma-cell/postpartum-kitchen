Caraway Tea

A warm seed tea that settles a bloated tummy — the after-dinner cup.
Time to make about 10 min
Keep the seeds take a second steep
When to drink After meals — especially when you feel bloated or windy.
Why this heals you
  • Caraway — a classic carminative seed: eases bloating & wind after meals
  • Same seed family as fennel — a traditional nursing ally
  • Warm and gentle on a tender gut
  • Caffeine-free

Caraway is a kitchen spice at tea doses and a traditional nursing seed. Herbs move milk far less than feeding often does — this cup earns its place by settling dinner. .
