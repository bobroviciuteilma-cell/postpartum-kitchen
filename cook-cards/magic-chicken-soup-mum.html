No-Water Magic Chicken Soup

No water added — a sealed pot lets cabbage, apple & onion melt into the chicken and make their own golden broth.
Time to make about 1 hour, mostly the pot's work
Keep fridge ~4 days · freezes well
When to eat Made for low, run-down days — a deeply concentrated broth. Sip the leftovers warm through the day.
Why this heals you
  • A whole chicken, gently steamed in its own juices — protein & collagen, nothing diluted
  • No added water — the cabbage, apple & onion release their own intense golden broth
  • Red dates & ginger — the warming confinement pair
  • Gentle on a low appetite — the sick-day pot

Everything in the pot is everyday food while feeding. The chilli lives in the sauce, not the pot — it goes on your bowl only if you fancy it. .

    Method
    1
    Layer the pot

    In a heavy pot: onion, apple, napa cabbage, red dates, ginger, salt — then sit the whole chickens on top. No water goes in.

    2
    Seal & cook

    Cover tightly and cook on low. The sealed steam pulls a golden broth out of the veg — that IS the soup.

    The lid stays on. Every peek lets the broth escape as steam — trust the pot until the timer rings.
    3
    Rest & serve

    Heat off. Lift out the tender chicken, spoon the veg into bowls, and ladle the self-made broth over. Scatter spring onion.

    4
    Ginger-scallion sauce

    Heat 1 tbsp oil until shimmering; pour it over the minced ginger & spring onion in a small bowl. Stir in 2 tbsp soy sauce. Chilli goes on Mum's bowl only, if she asked.
