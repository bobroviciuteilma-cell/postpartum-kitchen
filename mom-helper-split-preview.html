Beetroot Borscht

A ruby, iron-rich beef & beet soup — blood-building warmth in a bowl, finished with kefir & dill.
Serves 4–5
Time 2½ hrs
Keeps fridge 4 days / freeze
For you · a warm bowl, kefir on top
When to eat A warm bowl for lunch or dinner — especially in the first weeks, when you want iron & comfort together.
Why this heals you
  • Beetroot — folate & iron to rebuild the blood you gave to birth
  • Beef short rib — heme iron, protein & deep collagen
  • Cabbage & carrot — gentle fibre, vitamin C to unlock the iron
  • Kefir on top — a spoon of living probiotics for a healing gut

    Mum’s bowl

    Moringa + pumpkin seeds + kefir — from your pantry, stirred in at the end. Rosa gets this as her final step.

    Ingredients
    500 gBeef short ribbone-in, or stewing beef
    3 mediumBeetrootpeeled & grated or diced
    ¼ smallCabbagefinely shredded
    1Carrotgrated
    1Onionfinely diced
    3 clovesGarlicminced
    1 tbspTomato paste
    2Bay leaves
    2 tbspApple cider vinegaror juice of ½ lemon
    1.5 LBone brothor water
    1 small bunchFresh dillto finish
    to serveKefiror cultured sour cream
    Serve with a spoon of kefir or cultured sour cream, and dark rye or sourdough to dip. Tip: beetroot stains — grate it in an apron. Add the vinegar early to keep the soup a bright ruby, not brown.
    Method
    1
    Brown the beef · 10 min

    Brown the 500 g short rib in a little ghee in a big pot, then add the 1.5 L broth + 2 bay and simmer 1½–2 hrs until the meat is tender.

    2
    Soften the veg · 8 min

    In another pan soften the onion, carrot & beet with the 1 tbsp tomato paste until glossy and sweet.

    3
    Simmer together · 20 min

    Add the beet mixture, 3 cloves garlic and ¼ small shredded cabbage to the pot. Simmer 20 min until everything is soft.

    4
    Finish & serve

    Stir in the 2 tbsp vinegar (keeps it ruby) and most of the dill. Salt to taste. Ladle out, top with kefir and the rest of the dill.

    Make it PP Gold (for mum)

    Use bone broth as the base for extra collagen. Stir ½ tsp moringa into your bowl off the heat, and scatter 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds on top for zinc. A spoon of kefir adds probiotics & calcium. This version is for you.

    Good to know
    StoreFridge ~4 days; freeze in jars. Flavour deepens overnight.
    ColourThe splash of vinegar keeps it bright ruby, not brown.
    Why we say this
    Iron & folate in beetroot, and heme iron / collagen from long-simmered beef, are corroborated by ≥2 dated sources in the Knowledge Bank. The warming, blood-building framing is honestly labelled tradition.
