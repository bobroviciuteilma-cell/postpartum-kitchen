⚠︎ One caveat before you move anything

GitHub Pages serves files at their path. Moving pantry-preview.html into previews/pantry/ changes its live URL to …/previews/pantry/pantry-preview.html — so the links in this handover would break. The recipe links inside the previews are absolute and survive a move; only the preview's own address changes. Fix: do the move, then update the two URLs in the handover — or keep the pantry previews at root and only tidy the docs. Your call in the cleanup session.
