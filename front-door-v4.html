Why info-first

You were right — the read can't be specific before she tells us anything.

The hero stays general & true

"You don't need to push through" fits whatever season she's in — pregnant, postpartum, or just run down. A warm welcome, not a fake-personal claim.

Locate, then read

Two factual taps — her stage on the arc + feeding — make the read earned and specific ("week 7, breastfeeding → iron, minerals, extra protein"). No more blanket statements.

Factual ≠ emotional

Asking where she is (quick, factual) is fine; asking how she feels stays optional. Locating her is itself validating — it time-bounds the hard season.

Asked once

This is first-open only. After that the door already knows her stage and opens straight to the day's read.
