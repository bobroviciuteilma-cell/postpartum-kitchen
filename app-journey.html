Why this is the right UI

Every choice serves one of two jobs: lower her effort to near-zero, or make the warmth feel earned and premium.

One decision per screen

A wrecked mum can't triage a dashboard. Each screen resolves to a single obvious action — Apple's clarity applied to exhaustion.

Relief before homework

"Tonight is handled" lands first; the healing "add one thing" rides inside it. Relieve the burden, then offer the boost — never the reverse.

It owns 3am

A dark, whisper-only Rescue screen meets her at the worst hour and quietly queues the morning. That's loyalty you can't design into a recipe app.

Warmth that isn't a tech app

Linen, botanicals, a serif soul, amber + sage — an heirloom apothecary, not a SaaS dashboard. The warmth is the product.

Legible where it counts

Cormorant serif sings in the headlines; a warm humanist sans carries every label and button — readable at 3am on low brightness.

Zero-logging, zero shame

Progress shows what she added, not what she missed. No streaks, no red badges — guilt is fatal in postpartum.

The moat is visible

The upgrade diff, the synergy lines, ask-your-kitchen acting on her library — "this isn't ChatGPT" is something she can see, and it's the paywall.

It acts, not just answers

Every question and chart ends in a tap that changes tonight's pot or the shopping list. Knowledge becomes dinner, not reading.

Delegation with dignity

The Cook Card carries the why to the helper and gives honest outs. Mum provisions, the helper cooks — roles never blur.

Color is a language

Sage = her healing, amber = the one action, lavender = calm/baby. She learns the grammar without being taught it.
