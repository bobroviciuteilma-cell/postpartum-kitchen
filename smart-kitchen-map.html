What makes it a SMART kitchen — not a recipe app

A recipe app shows food. This kitchen knows who’s at her table, reads how she is today, boosts every meal from what she already owns, hands the cooking to someone else, learns from every plate — and proves what it saved her, in hours and dollars. Take any one part away and it’s just an app. Together, it’s a kitchen that thinks.

4 · It borrows hands
